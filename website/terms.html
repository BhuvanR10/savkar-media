FINANCIAL & PRODUCTION TERMS

💳 PAYMENT TERMS
  • 100% Advance Payment: 100% advance payment required before project onboarding and initiation.
  • Advance Monthly Retainers: Monthly retainers are payable in advance at the start of each billing cycle.
  • Suspension of Deliverables: Delayed payments may lead to immediate suspension of active deliverables.
  • Strict Commercial Exclusions: Third-party costs, ad spend, travel, and accommodation are strictly excluded from retainer fees.
🎬 REVISIONS & PRODUCTION POLICY
  • 2 Script Revisions: 2 script revisions are included within the standard project scope.
  • Post-Production Tweaks: Minor video changes and post-production tweaks are accommodated.
  • Scope Shifts & Reshoots: Major scope shifts, conceptual changes, or reshoots will attract additional commercial charges.
  • Minimum Term: Standard minimum 3-month commitment period applies to retainer engagements.
SECTION B : 5

TERMS & CONDITIONS (LEGAL GOVERNANCE)

5.1
Content Approval

The Client agrees to provide prompt written approvals or revision requests within 48 hours of asset submission. Delays in response may directly impact subsequent production schedules, social calendars, and guaranteed delivery timelines.

5.2
Client Responsibilities

The Client agrees to provide accurate brand assets, required account access credentials, product materials, and necessary project direction essential for uninterrupted execution.

5.3
Intellectual Property

Upon complete settlement of all agreed invoices, all final approved deliverables become the exclusive intellectual property of the Client. Savkar Media retains full proprietary ownership over internal workflows, raw project files, master templates, scripts, and production frameworks.

5.4
Portfolio Rights

The Client grants Savkar Media an irrevocable, royalty-free license to display and publish produced deliverables across agency portfolios, award entries, case studies, agency websites, and social media channels for self-promotional purposes.

5.5
No Guarantee Clause

Savkar Media applies industry best practices; however, the agency makes no warranties or explicit guarantees regarding direct sales volume, lead conversions, revenue figures, platform follower increments, or viral algorithm performance.

5.6
Confidentiality

Both parties mutually agree to treat all exchanged proprietary insights, marketing roadmaps, strategic documents, commercial terms, and operational data as strictly confidential.

5.7
Minimum Commitment Period

The Client commits to an initial minimum term of three (3) consecutive months as indicated in Section 1. In the event of early termination before the conclusion of this period, the Client remains legally liable for the payment of the remainder of committed service fees.

5.8
Termination

Following the initial commitment window, either party may terminate this Agreement by issuing a formal 15 days prior written notice.

5.9
Jurisdiction & Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India, and any legal proceedings or disputes arising shall fall under the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent courts in Bengaluru, Karnataka.
